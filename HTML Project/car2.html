BMW M5



The BMW M5 is a legendary high-performance luxury sedan that combines cutting-edge hybrid power with dynamic driving capabilities, embodying over 40 years of M-engineering excellence. The latest generation of the M5 pairs a high-revving TwinPower Turbo V8 engine with an integrated electric motor to deliver a combined system output of up to 727 hp and 1,000 Nm of torque, allowing breathtaking acceleration and supreme driving agility across a wide range of conditions. Its athletic design features boldly flared wheel arches, sculpted aero details, and distinctive M-specific lighting, while the luxurious interior offers adaptive sport seats, ambient lighting, premium Merino leather trim, a flat-bottom M steering wheel, and the BMW Curved Display with advanced iDrive controls for an immersive cockpit experience. With advanced M xDrive all-wheel drive, adaptive suspension, and a suite of high-tech features such as a Bowers & Wilkins surround sound system and four-zone climate control, the M5 effortlessly balances comfort, technology, and performance. Whether on everyday roads or the racetrack, the BMW M5 stands as a benchmark in the performance sedan segment, offering exhilarating power, refined luxury, and cutting-edge innovation.
